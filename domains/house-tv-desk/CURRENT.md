## Chat sparks (pick any · keep it casual)

### 1) Entertainment on demand
Ask Desk to sketch a **preview → accept** path for house media: e.g. “suggest three films we already have, I pick one, *then* I open Kodi myself.”  
Or: “propose a Friday night setup I can approve line by line.”  
You never hand the remote to the model. You preview the proposal in chat (or a future propose-CURRENT), then *you* accept.

### 2) Smart shopping, but make it technical-cozy
Curious how this stack could grow? Ask for a **grocery analysis methodology** for a smarter list — still human-owned files, still propose-only:  
“How would CURRENT + chat turn receipts or ‘what’s in the fridge’ notes into a shopping list I approve?”  
Think gentle data hygiene, not a surveillance fridge.

### 3) Mail → calm databases
Want a **technical demo sketch** for syncing convenient little databases from **Outlook / Gmail / whatever inbox you actually use**?  
Ask: “How would we capture email into plain files Desk can propose over — without the model sending mail?”  
Inbound notes → folders you can `cat`. That’s the vibe.

### 4) “Tell someone what we built”
Imagine showing a friend or family member *this* screen: chat left, living CURRENT right, and you say *I* decide what sticks.  
Ask Desk: “In two sentences, how do I explain this system to someone who’s never heard of Mechanicall?”  
Or: “What would it feel like to demo this on Client-one without the jargon?”
