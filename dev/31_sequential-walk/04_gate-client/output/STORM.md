## Perspectives (research, not a picker)

**Practitioner.** A sitting tool that meant the four claims would open on *this* folder, put a composer on the work, and return a schema the human can accept or throw away. What shipped is a sideloaded kiosk: Home prints a pre-bound demo path; Plan and Draft reprint CURRENT.md; Draft Chat points at `http://100.90.85.68:11434` and hung past 20s with *Waiting for a reply. This is not Yes.*; Decide is Approve/Reject on an empty room; Client Run is `cat CURRENT.md`. The one live “query” path did not answer. The one live “directory” control is a string box. That is not a seat a practitioner can sit on for an afternoon of work.

**Skeptic.** The app is honest about what it refuses (Opening is not Yes; Save is not Yes; Run is not Yes; Rooms do not approve). Honesty is not the product claim. Six protocol tiles plus a hamburger labeled Rooms do not become a chat app because Slack used the word first. A lavender Chat chip you can turn off until the screen says *Show Fields or Chat.* is an admission that chat is optional chrome on a form. A host field you can edit is an admission that the model is not the product. A two-tap Why dialog that we cancelled is a lock on a museum, not evidence the museum is a workspace.

**Product-law.** The pocket’s own CURRENT **Rejects** “a chatbot with no plan” and “model-as-authority.” The demo APK is labeled *not Mechanicall product.* That is internally consistent as a protocol face. It is a fail against the four-part definition this walk was ordered to use. Product-law here is CURRENT.md as the only law of the sitting — and the sitting’s law is a demo pocket whose Next is `name-the-outcome`, while the operator tree’s Next is `people-app-phone-seat`. Two CURRENTs. The phone is not a projection of the desk’s work. Bind did not refuse the operator-tree *name*; it said the folder was not on the phone. The law is a file. The app shows the file. Showing the law is not bridging to the work the law is supposed to govern.

**Client / sitter.** You open a logo. You are lectured. You are shown a path you did not pick. LAST SAID: *You have not said yes yet.* Tiles: Plan, Draft, Decide, Receipt, Bind, Client. None of them are a person, a thread, or a file of yours. Plan is six words in huge type. Draft Chat hangs. Decide asks if you are sure you want to approve *nothing you can read on that screen*. Receipt: *No decision recorded yet.* Client reprints the lecture as markdown. The sitter who wanted a phone seat for people-app + mate/ is looking at a protocol museum of a different Next (`name-the-outcome`). The sitter who wanted to talk to a local model is looking at a desk IP that did not answer. The sitter who wanted *their* folder is looking at `/storage/emulated/0/mechanicall-pocket`.

**Newcomer.** Standard chat apps: inbox or thread list, last message, composer pinned, send, a model or a person on the other end. This app: splash logo, status cards, six rooms, hamburger of the same six rooms plus four filenames. The Chat closet is under a form and can be hidden. Send does not produce a bubble; it produces a wait sermon, then a hang. There is no one to talk to. There is no folder to browse. There is a Confirm button we were forbidden to press, which is the only path that would change the plan — so the only consequential act is a human Yes on a two-tap gate that does not show the work.

## Article

The phone is a protocol museum with a chat closet that times out.

Live, 18:30–18:34 on an SM-A336B that `get-state` called `device`, the last walker opened Decide and found two pills on cream. No plan on that screen. No draft. No file. It is not.

Against claim 1 the app deploys a *host field* and a Send that entered a wait state. It does not, live, query a local model and return structured support. Score **1** for the whole app because the closet exists and was tried; 0 would be a lie about Draft, 2 would be a lie about the hang.

Against claim 4 the person is lectured at every door and pointed at CURRENT.md. A bridge would put them in conversation with the work in the folder. This puts them in front of the protocol, with a lock they must two-tap, a receipt that records nothing, and a client that reprints the law. Score **1**.

Rooms do not rescue this. Naming the tiles Plan and Draft does not make them a session. Opening the APK is not Yes, and it is also not a sit. The honest empty receipt is the most true screen in the app: nothing happened, and the machine did not pretend.
