## Screen grammar (post-millwork)

1. **Busyboard (home after Yes)** — one Next in big type. Two feet: **I did it** / **Not yet**. No lamp museum.  
2. **Mum gate (Decide)** — draft restated; Why required; green **Yes** / grey **Not yet**. Copy energy: “You have not said yes yet.”  
3. **Track (after Yes)** — optional micro-motion: stamp / whistle / GO — then quiet. Don’t animate forever.  
4. **Receipt** — till-style strip: when, Yes/Not yet, Next, short trail. Forwardable file.

## Colour / type (safe steal)

- Cream paper + graphite ink (keep from sit, drop photographic rack)  
- One accent for **suggestion** (violet) vs **active** (amber/green)  
- Neo-brutalist Yes button: thick border, press-shadow, thumb-sized  
- Avoid: IsolatedDark CRT, ALPHA/BETA/GAMMA banks, JOIN/WAKE as chrome

## Anti-inspo

- Pushary lock-screen Approve alone (no readable plan)  
- ChatGPT skin with fake Confirm  
- Millwork photo collage as the face  
- Rubber-stamp dialogs on Open / Bind / Send
