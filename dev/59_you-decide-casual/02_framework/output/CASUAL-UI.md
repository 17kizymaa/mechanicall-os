## 0. Who is who (do not invert)

| | Mum (she) | Son (he) |
|--|-----------|----------|
| House | Holds judgment. Rare Yes. Not yet is enough. Does not have to run the tools. | Goes, drafts, fetches, comes back. Does not rewrite the house rules. Does not nag. Does not hide. |

## 1. Must (face)

1. **She decides. He runs.** Opening the sit is not Yes. Bind, Send, FILES, GATE, JOIN, open are not Yes. Only Decide publishes. Models never approve. Silence is not her Yes.
2. **He puts work on the table.** Draft writes `PROPOSE-CURRENT.md`. CURRENT bytes do not change. The draft is readable in the language of the house, not a workshop dump.
3. **He comes back with what happened.** Receipt is what he did. Empty is empty. No performance of agree.
4. **He asks before it is irreversible.** One ask: Decide (two-tap + Why). Chores do not ask.
5. **Not yet is enough.** Leaving Decide without Yes keeps Next. He does not sulk and apply it anyway.
6. **Language of the house, not the workshop.** Not a terminal. Not a VST chassis. Not plugin / CRT / millwork / banks / wells as the words she meets.
7. **Care is refuse, not nag.** Preflight refuses danger. Copy does not hustle her into a tap. No streaks. No “just confirm.”
8. **One household, one plan.** One Next. One bound folder. Not this operator tree. Not dual-CURRENT.
9. **Tomorrow the papers are still there.** Chat dies. Files she can `cat` remain. The sit shows those files, not a chat log.
10. **No CLI required of her.** The toolbox stays his. The sit is how she meets him.

## 6. Visual grammar (toolkit unnamed)

A human pins native views / Compose / WebView / other **later**. This section is the relationship, not the widget kit.

**Must look like**

- A house of papers, not a DAW plugin and not a terminal.
- One household plan visible. Rooms are rooms, not a verb museum.
- The stamp is visually the only irreversible ask. Chores are quieter than the stamp.
- Draft and plan are distinguishable: draft is on the table; plan is the house rule.
- Receipt can be empty without decorating emptiness.
- Bound folder name is visible after the door. Operator tree is not.

**Must not look like**

- SitRackView chassis / FACE-SPEC rack (bevel, amber LCD, bank strip, trance-gate) as the *face*.
- A CRT tube as the only way to read the plan.
- IsolatedDark slabs as “dests.”
- Cartoon mum, family stickers, large-type millwork.
- Chat bubbles as the plan.
- A second app (Play One Next stranger shell) this halt.

Phosphor-as-bug stays rejected: we are not hunting glow; we are dropping **workshop language**. If a later visual Next wants warmth, it is not this file’s job to smuggle FACE-SPEC back in.

Peek vs room: a short read on the table is allowed. The whole file is a room she can enter. Do not put preview paging on a chassis glass.
