# What the app is actually worth

**When:** 2026-08-16 sitting was going to prove this. It cancelled. The worth did not.  
**Not a valuation.** No TAM. No Play Store. No “AI PM market.”  
**Not law.** Next is still `mobile-planning-demo`.

---

## One sentence

**The app is worth the first time someone can say “I didn’t say yes” — and the machine has to live with that.**

Everyone else is selling the opposite: an agent that already did it, sorry, here’s a summary.

---

## What the app *is* (so worth has an object)

Not this git tree. Not `aether help`. Not Hyprland. Not a store listing.

It is a **workshop table**:

- the plan is on the table (readable)
- the agent types the draft (point-and-click)
- one human is allowed to say Yes
- tomorrow the table is still there

Pocket chrome, ICM folders, panel TUI — those are tablecloths. The table is: **one Next, one Yes, one receipt.**

You already named the client as the planner. You already refused bubble-chat as the face. You already made Yes apply a visible PROPOSE, not a vibe. That is the app.

---

## What it is worth in three rooms

### 1. A friend (the lucky-kid share)

Worth **one night of sleep** and **one friendship that does not have to be the project manager**.

Right now the anxious person in every group chat is the CURRENT. They remember what we decided, they stop the mate who “already asked ChatGPT to email the venue,” they carry the plan in their head until they drop it. This app fires that person — kindly. The folder carries it. They can be a friend again.

If it works once, it is worth more than a year of ChatGPT Plus. Plus is £20/month for a memory that is not theirs and a Yes they never gave. You are giving them the Yes back.

### 2. A client sitting (the demo you actually designed)

Worth **the meeting**.

A live planning sitting is not a feature demo. It is the hour in which a project’s Next gets chosen. People pay £300–600 a day for a human to hold that hour. Solicitors charge more to write down what was agreed. You built a table where the client taps, the draft is visible, and you do not have to be the memory.

The 2026-08-16 sitting cancelled. That does not make the table worthless. It means you have not yet collected the only metric that matters: **did a person who is not you say Yes on their own plan, and open the receipt the next day?**

Until that happens, the app is worth *potential*. After it happens once, it is worth a practice. After it happens three times, it is worth teaching. Not a company. A practice.

### 3. The world (without getting drunk)

Worth **a brake pedal in a car everyone else is building without one.**

The next five years are agents that email, book, pay, merge, and “handle it.” The rare, rude, necessary product is the one that can **stop**. Your own doctrine already said this:

> The product must prove that it can stop one wrong action before it adds machinery for performing more actions.

That is not a slogan for investors. That is the whole market. Every other lab is adding accelerators. You built the pedal. Pedals do not look inspiring on a pitch deck. They are why people survive the drive.

You will not get unicorn money for a brake. You might get the kind of trust that lets a lost kid sit with someone’s mum’s paperwork and not become a monster.

## What it is *not* worth (so inspiration stays clean)

| Fake worth | Truth |
|------------|--------|
| Play Store revenue | Forbidden this Next; also the wrong prize |
| “Replace Jira / Notion / ChatGPT” | You replace the *anxious friend*, not the suite |
| A SaaS multiple | PRODUCT forbids that story as core |
| This repo as a gift | Friends cannot drink a monorepo |
| Your uncommitted folders | Capture. Not value. Value is one finished Yes |

The cost that already mattered is in your own decision tree: **your weeks**, not dollars. You spent them. The only way those weeks become worth something is a stranger’s (or a friend’s) Yes that is not yours.

---

## Why this should heat you up

You are not late to chatbots. You are early to **refusal as a gift**.

A lost kid cannot give people a better model. Frontier labs will always have more GPUs. You can give people something those labs are structurally unable to sell: **a machine that will not pretend they agreed.**

That is rare. That is yours. That is enough to walk toward.

Inspiration is not “imagine a million users.” Inspiration is:

> Tomorrow morning they open one page and know what they decided — and that you did not decide it for them.

If that sentence does not move you, the app is the wrong life. If it does, you do not need a second product. You need one table, one folder, one person who is not you.

---

## The worth test (approve this, not a vibe)

The app is worth building if you will do **one** of these:

1. Sit with one person and their folder until they tap Yes or Not yet — and leave a receipt they open without you.  
2. Close the phone Next so the table is not a decoration.

Worth is not a feeling you wait for. Worth is a Yes you have not collected yet.
