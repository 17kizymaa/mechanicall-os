## The picture worth walking toward

A person has a **folder of work**. They do not learn `aether`. They copy a small workspace. One agent reads the right files. They see the plan. The agent drafts. They say **Yes** or **Not yet**. Tomorrow they open a receipt.

That is already designed:

```text
01_bind → 02_show-plan → 03_propose → 04_gate → 05_do → 06_receipt
```

The workspace *is* the app. Mechanicall’s extra — the one that makes this *yours* — is that Yes lands in **their** `CURRENT.md`, not in a dashboard and not in this operator repo.

You do not need a new identity. You need to keep walking the stack you already built.

---

## Why the five layers are a gift

| Layer | What it protects | Why that is good |
|-------|------------------|------------------|
| **0 Identity** | Who we are (`AGENTS.md`, principles, this skill) | The agent does not wake up as a generic chatbot. It wakes up as Mechanicall: one Next, silence ≠ Yes. |
| **1 Routing** | Where to stand (`CURRENT.md`, factory `CONTEXT.md`) | Attention has a door. You are never “working on the whole company.” |
| **2 Stage contract** | What *this* folder is allowed to do | Inputs / Process / Outputs. Cheap to edit. You steer by changing a page, not by retraining a swarm. |
| **3 Factory** | How we always write (`PRODUCT`, SPEC, style) | Configure once. Every new run inherits the voice and the refusals. |
| **4 Working** | The actual product of the turn (`output/`) | You can `cat`, edit, or reject it. Chat can die. The folder remains. |

This is the same idea as Mechanicall’s pitch, said in filesystem:

> You decide. AI does the point-and-clicking — under a plan you can read and a yes only you can give.

ICM gives the *plan you can read* a shape (numbered folders). Mechanicall gives the *yes only you can give* a gate (`aether approve`). Together they are stronger than either slogan alone.

## Pros that are already true on this disk

**The architecture is visible.** Sixteen `dev/` folders are a diary of real work, not a hidden orchestrator. `08_v0.2-control-layer` is literally where the product was born. Anyone can walk in cold and see the story.

**One agent is enough.** This repo already rejected swarm-as-architecture. Jake’s limit (ICM does not love real-time multi-agent) matches Mechanicall’s limit (models never approve). That is alignment, not a compromise.

**Human gates are cheap.** You said “proceed to 02” and classify appeared. You can edit `classify.md` before any `git mv`. That loop is the product feeling we want other people to have.

**Layered load keeps the model honest.** We did not dump 163 receipts into one prompt to classify the tree. We read the inventory + LAB-STATUS + a directory listing. The stack *worked* in this conversation.

**Factory vs product is already named.** `25` emits a copyable workspace. `26` understands this repo. Root `aether` + `CURRENT.md` stay the law. We did not try to turn mechanicall-os into `01_bind/`. That restraint is a strength.

**The phone demo is the same loop in chrome.** Visible CURRENT, propose-only editor, Yes / Not yet, receipts. Kotlin is a face. `bin/aether` is the law. When you tell that story, desk and pocket agree.

**Portable.** A complete ICM workspace is a folder tree. Copy it. Open it with a different model next year. No vendor runtime is the architecture.

**Mechanical work stays mechanical.** `scaffold-icm.py`, `aether`, pocket scripts — the model does not pretend to be `cp`. That keeps the magic where it belongs: judgment, not file moves.

## The positive outcome (one north star)

**Name:** a person-with-a-folder can walk the six stages and stay in charge.

**Already have:** discovery, stage map, protocol CLI, Yes/Not-yet law, a pocket face that *projects* the same law, a classify map that tells an honest story about this repo.

**Do not need:** Play Store, OpenHands-as-identity, a second Next, re-rooting the product as numbered stages.

**Feel of done:** they open `06_receipt/output/RECEIPT.md` the next morning and know what was Next, what was Yes, and what files moved — without asking the chat to remember.

That is a manifestable outcome. It is small enough to finish. It is large enough to be the product.
