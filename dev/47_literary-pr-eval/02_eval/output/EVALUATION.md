## 2. Why the metaphors want you to reshape

They want the **center of gravity** moved.

### 2.1 From factory diary → constitution

Six weeks of `dev/NN_*` made Mechanicall look like a **method for building with agents** (ICM + sit chrome + STORM). The essay wants Mechanicall remembered as a **separation of powers**:

Model generates · Agent executes bounded work · Plan names the legitimate objective · Mechanicall records the transition · Human confers authority · Receipt proves it.

That is attractive because it is true of the *intent*. It is dangerous because it would license **stopping the sit, the millwork, the doorbell, the testers pack** on the grounds that those are “not the constitution.” They are not the constitution. They are the *face that makes the constitution usable by a person who will not learn `aether`*. `43` CONS already named the unpaid picture: a person has a folder, sees the plan, says Yes, opens a receipt tomorrow. The literary PR describes that picture in adult language. It does not collect it.

### 2.2 From “one Next” as project-management → “one Next” as anti-chaos

The essay says infinite intelligence produces infinite branches. That is exactly the journey-cons calendar (protocol-first → package-alpha → server-ops → multi-surface → people-app → phone → VST → STORM → testers → distro-gate → rack). The metaphor wants a **stricter identity**: research may fan out; **commitment may not**.

Take-forward: keep dual-Next Reject; keep propose-current as the only recant path.  
Do not take-forward: freeze the calendar so hard that blocked physical receipts (LTE, Play, a stranger Yes) get replaced by another essay.

### 2.3 From ICM-as-architecture → ICM-as-factory-under-authority

Jake’s paper (the one this repo’s meta-agent skill cites) says folders *are* agent architecture. Mechanicall **uses** that. The literary PR says Mechanicall **is not** an agent framework. Both can be true if you keep the split:

- **ICM** = how *this repo* builds (numbered `dev/`, CONTEXT.md, halt on `output/`).
- **Mechanicall** = how a *bound folder* authorizes a transition (CURRENT, preflight, human Yes, receipt).

The reshape-pressure is to collapse them: either Mechanicall becomes “just ICM with a nicer README” (EdubaWare’s lane), or ICM becomes “bureaucracy around Yes” and you stop numbering stages. History says both collapses have already been attempted (park-sit-edubaware propose; 40-line conclusions as the job while LTE was BLOCKED).

### 2.4 From sit millwork → “governed transition” as the only UI

The essay’s ideal experience is PRODUCT’s pitch. On the A33 that pitch is supposed to *look like*: Bind a folder → Plan is CURRENT on glass → Draft is a proposal → Decide is Yes+Why → Receipt. The millwork is not the doctrine. The millwork is how a non-developer **notices** the doctrine.

Take-forward: every plate must answer “which clause of Inference-is-not-authority is this surface?” If a pixel does not, it is leftover (already Reject: leftover-Compose-millwork-as-SOS).

### 2.5 The hidden reshape: “ambient and effortless” vs inspectable

That sentence licenses **EdubaWare-shaped UX** (cloud workbenches, MCP, “meeting becomes the chat”) *as long as files remain truth*. `43` PROCESS already warned: a blocked physical step is replaced by a new face. Beautiful-ambient is the most tempting new face.
