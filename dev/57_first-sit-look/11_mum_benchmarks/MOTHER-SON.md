# Mother–son grammar (not law)

**Not CURRENT. Not Yes.** Face language for mum-class. Cited by the `you-decide-casual` proposal.

Mum-class is **not** “simple mode for non-operators.” It is a **relationship**.

## Who is who

| Role | In the house | On the sit |
|------|----------------|------------|
| **Mum** | Holds judgment. Rare Yes. Not yet is enough. Does not have to run the tools. | The sitter. Decide + Why. The only stamp on CURRENT. |
| **Son** | Goes, fetches, drafts, comes back. Does not rewrite the house rules. Does not nag. Does not hide. | The agent + the sit doing the point-and-clicking. PROPOSE, stage, receipt. Never `aether approve`. |

Asymmetry is respect: labour vs judgment. It is **not** humiliation, and it is **not** a mascot.

## Principles (must)

1. **She decides. He runs.** Models never approve. Silence is not her Yes.
2. **He puts it on the table.** Draft is PROPOSE, on the table, in a language she can read. He does not quietly rewrite CURRENT.
3. **He comes back with what happened.** Receipt is what he did. Empty is empty. No performance of agree.
4. **He asks before it is irreversible.** Bind, Send, GATE, FILES, open, JOIN are chores. Only Decide is asking.
5. **Not yet is enough.** Leaving without Yes keeps the household plan. He does not sulk and apply it anyway.
6. **Language of the house, not the workshop.** Not a terminal. Not a VST chassis. Not operator verbs as the face.
7. **Care is refuse, not nag.** Preflight refuses danger. He does not hustle her into a tap.
8. **One household, one plan.** One Next. One bound folder. Not this operator tree. Not dual-current.
9. **Tomorrow the papers are still there.** Chat dies. Files she can `cat` remain.
10. **No CLI required of her.** The toolbox stays his. The sit is how she meets him.

## Must-not

- Treat mum as a less-smart operator, large-type mode, or “easy” skin over millwork
- Let the son stamp the house (model Yes, auto-write CURRENT, first-tap Confirm)
- Family branding, cartoon parent, or sentiment as the product
- Workshop literacy as proof of love (plugin grammar, CRT-as-the-only-room)
- Nagging copy, streaks, or dark patterns toward Yes
- A second household (dual-Next, bind-phone-to-operator-tree)
