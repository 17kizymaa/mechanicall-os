## Offer

Go to `anphuni.com/you-decide`.
Bring one messy or stalled digital project.
The door **mints** an unguessable ticket URL (`/you-decide/t/<secret>`). The link is the ticket. No login.

That page:

- standing paper CI element: **NOT ACTIVE** proposal (brief / proposed Next / decision boundary); copy/download; Generate/LLM **following**
- **drop in** — public, no login, **8 MiB**, lands in self-host `inbox/`
- **zip slot out** — empty until a later Yes; download ≠ Yes

Drop, download, and submit are **not** Mechanicall Yes.

After a later Yes (not this halt): one short project brief, one proposed Next, an explicit decision boundary, one bounded piece of approved work, the zip on **this same ticket**, one truthful receipt, and one following proposal that remains inactive until approved.

No hosted per-user 500MB quota. No sequential public `T-1` ids.
