## Findings

### A. What deep research applications actually produce

Deep research (OpenAI, Perplexity, Gemini, LangChain Open Deep Research, GPT-Researcher, Stanford STORM, LangChain local-deep-researcher) is a **writer**. The human reads a finished document. The planner’s questions are internal. There is usually **no Yes on a live plan**. LangChain even *retired* human-in-the-loop plan approval from the default Open Deep Research graph; the current path is Submit → report.

That is why a picker was protocol-wrong: it copied the *impatience* in 09:06 and ignored what “deep research” means in the tools named.

### C. What the 2025–2026 market actually ships

Two different products, often confused:

1. **Deep research** — report. Covered above.
2. **Coworker / computer-use apps (2026)** — you open a window; the agent proposes steps; **Allow/Deny** before consequential acts; **files remain**.

Closest documented local apps to Mechanicall’s locked seat:

- **Open WebUI Computer (`cptr`)** — plan mode, Allow/Deny on tools, deliverables as files in a workspace folder, git as the diff, chat as replay. Docs: “Approve the plan, not the keystrokes.” Closest loop match. Workstation harness, not a pocket Domain.
- **OpenWorker (Andrew Ng, Jul 2026, Tauri)** — desktop icon; breaks work into steps; checks in before send/shell/calendar; files + transcripts. Strongest downloadable **seat-shaped** desktop.
- **Pipali (Khoj, Tauri)** — desktop coworker, approval for unsandboxed commands; models via their cloud.
- **Cloud HITL** (Lindy, StackAI, Copilot Studio) — real Yes buttons, usually in Slack/email. Authority lives in their cloud. Not local-first.
- **Chat apps you open** (Open WebUI, AnythingLLM desktop, Khoj, Obsidian Copilot) — real applications. Opening chat is not Yes. No live plan as the authority object.

**The remaining hole:** almost nobody ships a phone/desktop seat whose **only** authority is a human tap on a **plan that is a file**, with a **receipt that is also a file**, independent of chat. That hole is exactly what this repo has been specifying since 3 August. It is incomplete, not uninvented.

## STORM perspectives (then one conclusion)

**Product-law.** The application is already named: the seat. Incomplete. CLI is not it. Faces (panel, Tauri, APK, URL, distro) are glass, not new products.

**Practitioner (bills).** A client does not buy a protocol, a factory, or a definition turn. They buy an hour in which a plan is visible and they (or you, for them) tap. If that window does not exist, the months of work do not invoice.

**Skeptic.** They locked “one application” on 3 August and have spent six weeks opening new Nexts that are projections of it. SeatMate’s own identity says it is not an app. 09:06 is them noticing the unpaid cheque.

**Client.** They open one window. They see what is decided. They tap Yes or they don’t. Tomorrow they can open a page that still says so. They never join the operator’s tailnet as the operator.

**Newcomer.** This is not ChatGPT, not Jira, not a Linux distro hobby. It is a partner that will not pretend they agreed.

## Explicit conclusion of wants

**Mechanicall is one application: the seat.**

You open it. The live plan (`CURRENT.md` of *that* project) is on the screen. The agent drafts a change you can see. You tap **Yes** or **Not yet**. Work happens only after Yes. A receipt remains in that project. The engine is `aether` + files. Phone, desktop, web, and a bootable stick are the **same application on different glass**, not a suite of products and not a folder you “gift.”

That sentence is not a new idea. It is the 3 August locked verdict + the PRODUCT pitch + the seat README + the 16 August phone sitting + the 10 August “law not stack” anti-overfit, collapsed. The work since late July (protocol, panel, pocket APK, people-app refuse-operator, events, tests) is the **engine and the glass of that one application**. It is paid only if that window is what a person opens.

What is *not* the want (because they have already rejected it in logs):

- Selling the container (“a folder that’s yours”) as the product.
- Selling a developer-bench slogan as the product.
- A CLI lifestyle for casual users.
- Bubble chat as the face.
- A second parser of CURRENT (Kotlin clone, cloud plan store).
- Ritual Yes because the protocol expected it.
- A multiple-choice identity exercise in place of the application.

09:06’s “something else” is not a fifth product. It is **the seat, standardised** — one thing you can point at — after a month of Nexts that were faces of it. “Reward late July” means: stop throwing the protocol away, and stop pretending each face is a new company.

**Market position (so the conclusion is not isolation):** 2026 coworker apps (Computer, OpenWorker) prove the loop sells. Cloud HITL proves the Yes button sells. Deep research proves reports are a different product. Mechanicall’s distinct claim, already built in law and only partly in UX, is that **the plan and the receipt are files you can `cat` tomorrow.** That is the hole. Filling it is the application. Describing it again is not.
